# Code of Conduct

We pledge to keep participation respectful and harassment-free regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Be specific, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, protect private information, and respect maintainer scope decisions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. This compact policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
